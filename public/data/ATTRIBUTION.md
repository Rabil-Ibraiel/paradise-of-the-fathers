# HMML Syriac manuscript index

`hmml-syriac-index.json` is a compact selection of every record in the
2026-07-26 HMML Reading Room weekly dataset whose metadata identifies Syriac as
a language or writing system.

- Source: https://www.vhmml.org/dataPortal/dataset
- Schema: https://www.vhmml.org/dataPortal/schema
- Creator: Hill Museum & Manuscript Library and the cataloguers acknowledged in
  each individual record
- Metadata license: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- Transformation: field selection, category mapping, and compact JSON
  generation by The Paradise of the Fathers project

The metadata license does not grant publication rights to manuscript
photographs. Each image remains governed by the owning repository and the
access statement attached to its HMML record.
